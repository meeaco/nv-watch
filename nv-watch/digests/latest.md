### [Dynamical-mean-field cluster-correlation expansion of NV$^-$ spin-echo decoherence in a realistic substitutional-nitrogen bath](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02528)
- Chikara Shinei · arXiv:2611.02528 · quant-ph (cats: quant-ph) · submitted 2026-11-02 · relevance score 4 · found by arXiv-API
- Abstract: The Hahn-echo time $T_2$ of nitrogen-vacancy (NV$^-$) ensembles in nitrogen-rich diamond follows the empirical relation $T_2\cdot$[N] = 160 $μ$s ppm, independent of field between 2 and 30 mT. Cluster-correlation-expansion (CCE) calculations that treat the Overhauser field of the surrounding P1 centres as a frozen, sampled mean field overestimate it at high field by up to a factor of two, an excess recently attributed to additional dark defects. We show that the excess is largely an artefact of freezing the field. The P1 centres outside each cluster flip-flop in pairs that conserve their total $S_z$, generated by kinetic Monte Carlo at golden-rule rates from the dipolar Hamiltonian without adjustable parameters, and each cluster is propagated in the resulting time-dependent field. This dynamical mean field interpolates between the static-mean-field and mean-field-free limits. For a $^{14}$N P1 bath it reduces $T_2\cdot$[N] at 30 mT from 264 $\pm$ 16 to 188 $\pm$ 12 $μ$s ppm and gives 167 $\pm$ 11 $μ$s ppm at 10 mT, 1.7 and 0.4 standard deviations from experiment, with no dark spins. Letting each centre jump independently gives 147 $\pm$ 9 $μ$s ppm at 30 mT; the correlation of the flip-flop partners lengthens $T_2$ by 20-30 percent at high field. The Overhauser field is neither frozen nor motionally narrowed: 68-83 percent of its autocorrelation survives 200 $μ$s. At 2.5 mT, where the P1 electron and nucleus hybridise, $T_2$ remains below experiment for any choice of rates, so part of the field dependence remains ($T_2$ rises by a factor 1.69 $\pm$ 0.07 instead of 2.15 $\pm$ 0.08 from 2.5 to 30 mT).

### [Quartet state of the neutral nitrogen-vacancy center in diamond and optimized spin readout under near-infrared two-photon excitation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03343)
- Nikoletta Jegenyes et al. (7 authors) · arXiv:2611.03343 · quant-ph (cats: quant-ph) · submitted 2026-11-02 · relevance score 4 · found by arXiv-API
- Abstract: Photoionization of the excited nitrogen-vacancy center (NV-) in diamond leaves the neutral defect in a metastable quartet state of unknown energy. Using two-photon optically detected magnetic resonance with a tunable femtosecond laser, we resolve its onset in the spin contrast at 1.23 +/- 0.02 eV and, with the ground-state threshold from the same setup, place the quartet 0.54 +/- 0.04 eV above the neutral ground state. The threshold sets the optimum excitation for spin readout at 1060-1075 nm, in the near-infrared tissue window, with 26-37% higher shot-noise-limited sensitivity than at 1030-1040 nm.

### [Floquet engineering of quantum relaxation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01325)
- Kang Xu et al. (6 authors) · arXiv:2611.01325 · quant-ph (cats: quant-ph) · submitted 2026-10-31 · relevance score 2 · found by arXiv-API
- Abstract: Dynamical decoupling suppresses decoherence by averaging selected interactions between a quantum system and its environment. Here, we extend this notion through a general Floquet formalism that maps periodic control onto relaxation and dephasing channels. Environmental fluctuations are decomposed into Floquet components that sample the noise spectrum at distinct frequencies, with coupling strengths determined by control-induced Floquet coefficients. We experimentally investigate this approach using a single nitrogen-vacancy center in diamond under multi-pulse control. By tuning the drive and detuning, we reshape the coupling to environmental fluctuations and create isolated operating points where the dominant decoherence channel vanishes, strongly suppressing Floquet dephasing. The resulting coherence enhancement follows the predicted Floquet coupling coefficients and can be shifted by modifying the control sequence. These results establish Floquet driving as a general framework for engineering transverse and longitudinal relaxation through the design of quantum control protocols.

### [Theoretical estimation of nonlinear response function and ultrafast optical spectroscopy of nitrogen vacancy centers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01326)
- Arunangshu Debnath · arXiv:2611.01326 · quant-ph (cats: quant-ph) · submitted 2026-10-31 · relevance score 0 · found by arXiv-API
- Abstract: The theoretical description of the ultrafast optical response of nitrogen-vacancy centers has traditionally been developed using the nonlinear response function formalism based on a displaced harmonic oscillator model. However, restrictive defect geometries that lead to rich phonon mode distributions can limit the validity of this model. Here, we extend this framework by incorporating state-dependent oscillator modes characterized by different frequencies. We demonstrate that, as a consequence, the nonlinear response function is modified by a straightforward correction term that can be evaluated using operator algebraic techniques. These modifications directly impact two-dimensional correlation spectroscopy, which serves as a vital characterization tool for color centers by resolving ultrafast correlated electron-vibrational dynamics.
