### [High Fidelity Single-NV Qubit Quantum State Tomography by Photoelectric Readout](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16502)
- Boo Carmans, Michael Petrov, Milos Nesladek · arXiv:2603.16502 · quant-ph (cats: quant-ph) · submitted 2026-10-21 · relevance score 6 · found by arXiv-OAI
- Abstract: The nitrogen-vacancy (NV) center in diamond is one of the advanced candidates for solid-state quantum computing at room temperature and has proven to be competitive in terms of gate fidelity and the availability of quantum error correction codes. Ambient solid-state qubits may therefore provide quantum computing architectures as an alternative to current solutions. The NV center has an associated electronic spin that is conventionally read out via photoluminescence. As an alternative, the electrical readout is not limited by the confocal resolution of quantum microscopes, which is required in the development of NV-based quantum processors to establish a scalable individual single-spin readout on the order of a few nanometers (necessary for strong dipole-dipole coupling). In this work, the performance of photoelectric readout versus traditional optical readout is investigated to perform Quantum State Tomography measurements on a single NV center. A state fidelity of 0.995 $\pm$ 0.006 is achieved using photoelectrical spin-state readout for state reconstruction, comparable to simultaneous optical measurements. We report photocurrent signal delays, which significantly slow down the photocurrent tomography. We develop a model describing their origin, and propose strategies for their mitigation.

### [Solution-phase fluorination of nanodiamond: near-surface NV$^-$ activation and spin relaxation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06605)
- Szabolcs Czene et al. (7 authors) · arXiv:2609.06605 · quant-ph (cats: quant-ph, cond-mat.mtrl-sci) · submitted 2026-10-22 · relevance score 5 · found by arXiv-OAI
- Abstract: Nanodiamonds hosting luminescent point defects, known as fluorescent nanodiamonds (FND), are a leading platform for quantum technology. The nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centre is the most intensively studied of these; its negatively charged state (NV$^-$) can serve as a qubit at room temperature, and its stability is governed by surface functional groups. We present two solution-phase fluorination routes for stabilising NV$^-$: direct C-F bond formation by decarboxylation with xenon difluoride via a radical mechanism, and the Balz-Schiemann reaction, which replaces surface amino groups with fluorine. The two routes were compared by infrared, X-ray photoelectron, energy-dispersive X-ray, Raman and photoluminescence spectroscopy. Both gave a high NV$^-$ fraction, up to $\sim$85% on average, which to our knowledge is among the highest reported for surface-terminated nanodiamonds of this size and, in particular, for fluorine termination. We attribute the stabilisation to the fluorination treatment, which removes acceptor-like carboxyl groups and forms C-F bonds that give the surface a positive electron affinity. Frequency-domain relaxometry shows that the fluorinated particles retain a long spin-lattice relaxation time, $733\pm56$ and $712\pm20$ $μ$s for the XeF$_2$ and Balz-Schiemann routes, several times the values reported for commercial HPHT nanodiamonds, although shorter than the $1173\pm123$ $μ$s of the as-received material. Fluorination thus activates near-surface NV$^-$ centres, which dominate relaxometric sensing, at the cost of a moderate shortening of $T_1$.

### [Cooperative control of dissipative qubits: steering along the fastest dissipative contraction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30073)
- Robert Weiß, Sandro Wimberger, David Guéry-Odelin · arXiv:2606.30073 · quant-ph (cats: quant-ph, physics.atom-ph) · submitted 2026-10-20 · relevance score 2 · found by arXiv-OAI
- Abstract: In the control of dissipative quantum systems, the slow relaxation modes usually set the ultimate manipulation timescale. We show that this bottleneck can be bypassed when dissipation itself is used as the engine of the transfer. For a qubit with non-unital, anisotropic Bloch relaxation characterized by the ratio $κ=T_1/T_2>1$, coherent control can only rotate the Bloch vector, whereas the bath alone can change its length. The fastest transfer between two thermal states therefore consists in steering the Bloch vector, at every instant, along the direction in which the bath contracts it most rapidly. This direction is obtained in closed form and lies slightly beyond the equatorial plane. With strong pulses, the resulting minimum transfer time is a fraction of $T_2$, instead of several $T_1$ for passive relaxation. With bounded fields, the Pontryagin maximum principle yields a bang--singular--bang synthesis whose singular arc is precisely this fastest-contraction direction, and the minimum time exceeds its strong-pulse limit by a correction inversely proportional to the field amplitude. We also determine the full time--energy Pareto front: moderate accelerations require little energy, and the time optimum lies at its steep, high-energy end. Off-axis targets are treated within the same framework; their only additional benefit is a shorter final rotation. These results apply directly to magnetic-resonance spins, nitrogen-vacancy centers and superconducting qubits, all of which operate deep in the strong-pulse regime.

### [Multiparametric quantum sensing of liquids using NV centers and tethered magnetic nanoparticles](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0344327)
- Johannes Fiedler, Martin Greve, Justas Zalieckas · APL Quantum · created 2026-10-03 · published 2026-10-02 · via OpenAlex · relevance score 9
- Abstract: We propose a new concept for non-invasive, multiparametric liquid analysis based on nitrogen-vacancy (NV) center magnetometry, relaxometry, and surface-tethered magnetic nanoparticles. Magnetic nanoparticles are anchored to a diamond surface via DNA strands, forming nanoscale mechanical oscillators whose thermally driven motion is strongly influenced by the surrounding liquid environment. The resulting time-dependent magnetic fields couple to near-surface NV centers and are detected via optically detected magnetic resonance or changes in spin coherence time. By spatially patterning the diamond surface with regions functionalized by DNA tethers of different lengths, sequences, or chemical modifications, a single liquid is mapped onto a high-dimensional quantum response vector rather than a single scalar observable. Changes in viscosity, molecular adsorption, or chemical interactions modify the dynamics of magnetic nanoparticles in a region-specific manner, enabling differential sensing across the surface. We outline the physical transduction mechanism, discuss relevant scaling relations, and assess experimental feasibility using established wide-field NV magnetometry and relaxometry methods. The proposed platform combines quantum sensing with surface heterogeneity, offering a versatile route toward parallel, label-free liquid characterization.

### [Spin-Uniform Nanodiamond Quantum Sensors for Reproducible Intracellular Thermometry](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsnanoscienceau.6c00068)
- Chihiro Suzuki et al. (11 authors) · ACS Nanoscience Au · created 2026-10-07 · published 2026-10-06 · via OpenAlex · relevance score 9
- Abstract: Abstract Nanodiamond quantum sensors have been regarded as promising probes for cellular thermometry, but their poor sensor-to-sensor reproducibility has hindered practical implementation. In cellular measurements, the resulting temperature-readout offsets can be mistaken for real cell-to-cell temperature differences, limiting quantitative comparison. Here, we show that spin-uniform nitrogen vacancy (NV)-rich nanodiamonds can be produced via postenrichment oxidative size reduction, which removes defective surface layers while improving crystallinity. Compared with nanodiamonds prepared by conventional mechanical milling before NV enrichment, the particle-to-particle spread in the temperature-sensitive D values was reduced by 3.7-fold. The improved NV homogeneity also enhanced spin properties: the optically detected magnetic resonance contrast─a key signal amplitude for sensitivity─increased by more than 50%. Together, these improvements reduce cell-to-cell dispersion in D-derived single-cell temperature readouts to below 0.7 K, enabling reliable temperature comparison across cells. This approach advances nanodiamond absolute thermometry toward organelle-scale intracellular temperature mapping and practical quantum sensing in biology and medicine.

### [Assessment of the physical and technological limits of brain-computer interfaces based on quantum magnetometry with NV centres in diamond](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23528989)
- José Israel Nadal Vidal, Antonio Ginés López · Zenodo (CERN European Organization for Nuclear Research) · created 2026-10-22 · published 2026-10-20 · via OpenAlex · relevance score 9
- Abstract: Background. Project Reload proposes a brain-computer interface based on nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centres in diamond, with an electron-nuclear hybrid memory, "emulated qubits", a 25-qubit processor and a prototype within 9 to 12 months. Objective. To determine which parts of the proposal are demonstrated, which are plausible and which are unsupported, and to quantify the distance between what is required and what has been demonstrated. Methods. Structured literature review (52 studies) and computational feasibility analysis: a record of the provenance of every input parameter, signal-to-noise, coherence, retention and error-correction models, simulation of dynamical decoupling, a 20,000-iteration timeline model and global sensitivity analysis. Results. With 1 s of integration, which assumes a sustained signal or the average of repeated events, reading a single neuron at 1 mm requires a sensitivity 75 to 297 times better than the best published for an NV ensemble, and 14 times better in the most favourable combination explored, if peak-to-peak field values are used as amplitudes; with peak amplitudes, the figures double. An isolated spike is detectable only up to a median of 10.7 µm. A logical error of 10-6 with the surface code requires 161 physical qubits per logical qubit. Coherent integration over 8 ms requires a T2 of 35.9 ms, 15 times the best value in bulk diamond; classical accumulation of repeated cycles does not have that requirement. Demonstrated quantum memory spans 1 to 60 s. Under the model's assumptions, the full route to a usable prototype has a median of 113 months and a limited laboratory demonstrator one of 18 months (probability of 4.4% of completion within 12). Conclusions. NV magnetometry of neuronal populations at short range is a defensible research line; non-invasive single-cell reading at millimetre scale, memory at psychological scale and a usable prototype within 12 months are not supported by the available evidence.

- Abstract: The work presents a comprehensive comparative study of diamond nanoparticles obtained by two fundamentally different methods – mechanical grinding of natural diamond (NND) and detonation synthesis (DND) – in their initial state and after thermal modification in air. The use of a combination of complementary high-resolution analysis methods allowed for the acquisition of reliable and reproducible data on the morphology and crystal structure of both the diamond core and the surface shell of the nanoparticles. The study revealed differences in the structure and morphology of the primary NND and DND particles. - Abstract: Kesirli Kuantum Dinamiği Altında Stratum Mimarisi: NV-Weyl-Majorana Karma Sistemlerinde Topolojik Koruma, Kuantum Hata Düzeltme ve Işınlanma Mehmet Keçeci Orcid: https://orcid.org/0000-0001-9937-9839 Özet/Abstract: Bu çalışmada, önceki çalışmamızda önerilen Stratum tabanlı karma (hibrit) kuantum mimarisi modelinin kesirli kuantum dinamiği altında genişletilmiş kapsamlı bir analizi sunulmaktadır. Stratum Modeli, elmastaki nitrojen-boşluk (NV) merkezlerini kontrol ve algılama katmanı, Weyl yarımetal arayüzlerini topolojik geçiş ve bilgi aktarım katmanı, Majorana sıfır modlarını ise içsel hata dirençliliği sağlayan topolojik hesaplama katmanı olarak tanımlayan üç katmanlı bir hiyerarşi önerir. Ancak bu mimarinin pratik uygulanabilirliği, katmanlar arası etkileşimlerin zaman içindeki sürecine doğrudan bağlıdır. Geleneksel analizlerde açık kuantum sistemleri Markovian (hafızasız) varsayımla modellenirken, gerçek NV merkezleri — özellikle yüzeye yakın olanlar — çevreleriyle güçlü hafıza etkili (non-Markovian) etkileşimler sergiler. Bu tür sistemlerin zaman süreci kesirli diferansiyel denklemler tarafından yönetilir ve çözümler Mittag-Leffler fonksiyonu E_{α,1}(−(t/τ)^α) cinsinden ifade edilir. Bu çalışmanın temel motivasyonu, üç katmanın tümünün tek bir kesirli parametre α tarafından eşzamanlı olarak kontrol edilebileceğini niceliksel olarak göstermektir. Bu hipotezi test etmek için beş bağımsız hesaplamalı adım gerçekleştirilmiştir: (i) NV spin-eko deneylerinden α parametresinin çıkarılması için deneysel doğrulama protokolü ve Akaike bilgi kriteri tabanlı model seçimi; (ii) kesirli gürültü altında yüzey kodunun hata toleransı eşiğinin yeniden hesaplanması; (iii) üç katmanlı Stratum mimarisine kesirli dinamik entegrasyonu; (iv) kesirli Landau-Zener geçişlerinde diabatik olasılıkların nicelendirilmesi; (v) NV→Weyl→Majorana zinciri üzerinden uçtan uca kuantum ışınlama sadakatinin hesaplanması. Sonuçlar, kesirli dinamiğin iki zıt zaman rejiminde farklı davrandığını ortaya koymaktadır. Kısa zaman ölçeğinde (t < τ), Markovian varsayım (α ≈ 1) genellikle daha iyi performans verir çünkü (t/τ)^α > (t/τ) eşitsizliği küçük α için başlangıçta daha hızlı bozunmaya yol açar. Ancak uzun zaman ölçeğinde (t ≫ τ), α < 1 için kuvvet yasası kuyruğu üstel bozunmaya göre çok daha yavaştır: t = 100τ anında α = 0.3 için NV ve Weyl katmanları α = 1'e göre 4.5 × 10⁴² kat, Majorana katmanı ise 1.0 × 10²¹ kat daha iyi koruma sergiler. Geçiş noktası t ≈ τ olup, bu, hızlı kuantum kapılar için α ≈ 1 ve uzun kuantum bellek/ışınlama protokolleri için α < 0.5 önerilmesini gerektirir. Ek olarak, α = 0.3 için yüzey kodu eşiği p_c'nin %1'den %3.16'ya yükseldiği (3.16× iyileşme) ve aynı hata toleransı için yaklaşık üç kat daha az fiziksel kübit gerektiği gösterilmiştir. Kesirli Landau-Zener geçişlerinde diabatik olasılık, v = 0.1 süpürme hızında α = 0.3 için 0.882, α = 1.0 için ise 0.534 olarak hesaplanmıştır. Kuasipartikül zehirlenme oranının α < 1 için γ_QP(t) ∝ t^(α−1) biçiminde azalması, hafıza etkisinin gürültü kaynağını da sürekli bastırdığını ortaya koymaktadır. Bu sonuçlar, kesirli dinamiğin Stratum mimarisinin uzun ömürlü protokolleri için zorunlu bir bileşen olduğunu ancak kısa kuantum kapıları için Markovian varsayımın yeterli olduğunu niceliksel olarak kanıtlamaktadır. Stratum Architecture Under Fractional Quantum Dynamics:Topological Protection, Quantum Error Correction and Teleportation in NV-Weyl-Majorana Hybrid Systems Abstract: In this study, a comprehensive extended analysis of the Stratum-based hybrid quantum architecture model proposed in our previous work is presented under fractional quantum dynamics. The Stratum Model proposes a three-layer hierarchy consisting of nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers in diamond as the control and sensing layer, Weyl semimetal interfaces as the topological transition and information transfer layer, and Majorana zero modes as the topological computation layer providing intrinsic error resilience. However, the practical applicability of this architecture is directly dependent on how inter-layer interactions evolve in time. While conventional analyses model open quantum systems with the Markovian (memoryless) assumption, real NV centers — especially those near surfaces — exhibit strong memory-affected (non-Markovian) interactions with their environments. The time process of such systems is governed by fractional differential equations, and the solutions are expressed in terms of the Mittag-Leffler function E_{α,1}(−(t/τ)^α). The primary motivation of this work is to quantitatively demonstrate that all three layers can be controlled simultaneously by a single fractional parameter α. To test this hypothesis, five independent computational steps were performed: (i) an experimental validation protocol for extracting α from NV spin-echo experiments and Akaike information criterion-based model selection; (ii) recomputation of the surface code error tolerance threshold under fractional noise; (iii) integration of fractional dynamics into the three-layer Stratum architecture; (iv) quantification of diabatic probabilities in fractional Landau-Zener transitions; (v) calculation of end-to-end quantum teleportation fidelity across the NV→Weyl→Majorana chain. The results reveal that fractional dynamics behave differently in two opposing time regimes. At short timescales (t < τ), the Markovian assumption (α ≈ 1) generally yields better performance because the inequality (t/τ)^α > (t/τ) leads to faster initial decay for small α. However, at long timescales (t ≫ τ), the power-law tail for α < 1 is much slower than exponential decay: at t = 100τ, the NV and Weyl layers exhibit 4.5 × 10⁴² times better protection for α = 0.3 compared to α = 1, while the Majorana layer shows 1.0 × 10²¹ times better. The crossover point is t ≈ τ, implying that α ≈ 1 is recommended for fast quantum gates and α < 0.5 for long quantum memory/teleportation protocols. Furthermore, the surface code threshold p_c was shown to increase from 1% to 3.16% for α = 0.3 (3.16× improvement), requiring approximately three times fewer physical qubits for the same error tolerance. In fractional Landau-Zener transitions, the diabatic probability at sweep rate v = 0.1 was calculated as 0.882 for α = 0.3 vs 0.534 for α = 1.0. The quasiparticle poisoning rate decreasing as γ_QP(t) ∝ t^(α−1) for α < 1 demonstrates that the memory effect also continuously suppresses the noise source. These results quantitatively demonstrate that fractional dynamics are an essential component for long-lived protocols in the Stratum architecture, while the Markovian assumption is sufficient for short quantum gates.
